# Press cover artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The final set contains English-language covers; Telex, 444, and Motor were retained at the user's request. Rejected Hungarian covers were deleted.

Shared direction for the seven replacements: one standalone 2D pixel-art game item sprite, portrait 3:4 front cover, flat straight-on orthographic view, no spine or thickness, opaque paper with a genuinely transparent margin. PS1 supermarket style, blocky pixel clusters, muted palette, large readable masthead, small barcode and issue number. All readable words must be English. No Hungarian text or publication names.

Retained covers used the same flat, transparent-background pixel-art direction:

- **444:** Independent newspaper, bold black masthead on white newsprint, yellow accent block, pixel city street image and editorial columns.
- **Telex:** Dark blue newspaper masthead with teal accent, white newsprint, pixel tram and city illustration and editorial columns.
- **Motor:** Car magazine, vivid red masthead, blue sports car on a winding road, charcoal and white typography.

The original full-resolution generated PNGs are stored in Sprites. Unity imports them at a 64-pixel maximum size with Point filtering, preserving the originals for future adjustments.
